The headliner
ScHoolboy Q is bringing Blank Face LP back to the stage

Ten years after Blank Face LP, ScHoolboy Q is marking the album with a limited anniversary tour, including a Washington, DC stop. It is not just another run of dates built around the newest release. The album itself is the event.

Anniversary shows hit differently because you know exactly what you are showing up for. You get the deep cuts that usually disappear from setlists, and the album gets to feel like a full experience again instead of a few songs mixed into everything else.

It also makes the decision easier. When the whole point of the show is an album you already love, there is less of that “maybe next time” energy.

The lesson for the group chat: when the entire premise is a record you already love, this probably is the next tour.

MetLife Stadium
BTS made an 82,000-person stadium feel weirdly personal
BTS opened the second North American leg of the ARIRANG World Tour with two nights at MetLife. The visuals were massive, but the synchronized light sticks, fan chants, and crowd rituals were what turned the show from a spectacle into a shared experience.

Fenway Park
Zac Brown Band sent 37,000 fans home with a free cruise
At the band’s 15th sold-out Fenway show, every eligible fan was offered a cruise for two. Nobody bought a ticket expecting to leave with a vacation, which is exactly why people will be talking about it for years.

From the archive
Elvis Costello is celebrating year 49 instead of waiting for 50
The new My Aim Is True box set includes 52 previously unreleased recordings, alternate takes, demos, and live performances. Skipping the obvious 50th-anniversary timing somehow makes the whole thing feel more like Costello.

DC show to know
Noname celebrates 10 years of Telefone
Noname plays the Howard Theatre this Sunday, August 9, for the tenth anniversary of Telefone. Her sharp writing and jazz-leaning production are built for a room where the audience can actually hear every line. Doors open at 7 p.m.; the show starts at 8.
